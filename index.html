Services

What we build

All services

Luxury residences

Custom homes and estates, from structure to finish. The Florida project published on this site is the 7,600-square-foot Lake Rodgers Waterfront Estate in Boca Raton, with a 160-foot deep-water dock.

Luxury renovations

Major renovations and condo work, including ground-up and gut-rehab projects the firm has published in Manhattan, Brooklyn, and Long Island.

Commercial

New construction and renovations for retail centers, senior housing, hotels, offices, and mixed-use developments. The published commercial projects are in New York, New Jersey, and Pennsylvania.

Tenant improvements

Interior build-outs and office fit-outs, in coordination with architects and designers. The published interiors are on Long Island.

Houses of worship

Churches, temples, and meeting houses — including work published for the Archdiocese of Rockville Centre and the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Construction management, as owner’s representative.

Owner’s representation

Construction management and consulting: value engineering, coordination with design and engineering teams, and on-time, on-budget delivery. The published proof is two client letters, from 2002 and 2010.

About

A construction practice that began in 1985.

Thomas Virzi, President of East Coast Construction, founded East Coast Construction Management in Garden City, New York, in 1985. He is a member of the Construction Management Association of America. The company later expanded into general contracting. The Florida office is in Boca Raton. The site says northeast operations run from Great River, New York.
