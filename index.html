About

A construction practice that began in 1985.

Thomas Virzi, President of East Coast Construction, founded East Coast Construction Management in 1985 after serving as Project Manager and Associate at Eastern Construction Management in Great Neck, New York. The firm later expanded into general contracting as East Coast Construction Group, specializing in new building construction and renovation projects of $30 million or less.

Mr. Virzi is a member of the Construction Management Association of America and acts as both a construction consultant and construction manager. His published project experience includes office buildings, hotels, retail centers, houses of worship, community and public buildings, luxury condominiums and residences, and senior housing.

East Coast Construction lists its Florida office in Boca Raton, and states that it currently operates its northeast operations from Great River, New York.
