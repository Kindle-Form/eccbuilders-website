FAQ

Questions owners ask before they call

Short answers. Where the firm has not published a fact, the answer says that.

  1. Home/
  2. FAQ
Is East Coast Construction licensed in Florida?

The site publishes Florida Certified General Contractor license #CGC1525184. The owner should confirm the license is still active. The public search is the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation license portal.

Where is the office, and how do I reach you?

The published office is 7700 Congress Avenue, Suite 1139, Boca Raton, FL 33487. Phone (561) 245-5044. Email avirzi@eccbuilders.com. The About page also says northeast operations run from Great River, New York. Office hours are not published.

What area do you serve?

The site describes a state-licensed commercial and residential contractor serving South Florida from West Palm Beach to the greater Miami area. The only Florida project published by name is in Boca Raton.

Are the New York projects Florida work?

No. Lake Rodgers Waterfront Estate in Boca Raton is the Florida project. The other case studies are the firm’s published Northeast record and are labeled Heritage. They are prior track record, not a South Florida project list.

Do you work as a general contractor, a construction manager, or both?

Both are published. The service pages describe building work — residences, renovations, commercial, tenant improvements, and houses of worship. Owner’s representation is published as construction management and consulting. Design-build is not named on the public site.

How does pricing work?

Call, email, or send the quote form with the project type, location, and square footage. The site does not publish a fee schedule, a rate card, or a standard contract form.

Can you work with our architect?

Tenant improvements are described as interior build-outs done in coordination with architects and designers. The site does not say whether an owner must bring an architect, or whether the firm hires one.

Who pulls the building permit?

That is not published. On a Florida project the permit is issued by the local building official — for a property inside the City of Boca Raton, that is the city building department — but the site does not say whether East Coast Construction, the owner, or the architect files the application.

How will I hear about progress?

The firm describes seamless communication as part of the client experience. A meeting cadence, weekly report, or photo update is not published.

Are you insured and bonded? What is your safety record?

Insurance, bonding, EMR, and a written safety program are not on the public site. Certificates are not offered for download here. Ask the office if you need them.

How does a trade partner get on the bid list?

Use the trade-partner form. It asks for the company, trade, license number if you have one, and whether you carry general liability and workers’ compensation. Required insurance limits are not published, and submitting the form does not promise an invitation to bid.

Are you hiring?

No open jobs are published. The careers page explains that, and it has a way to send interest for when the owner posts roles.

Answers that the public site does not settle — permits, insurance, update cadence — say so in the answer, instead of filling the gap.
