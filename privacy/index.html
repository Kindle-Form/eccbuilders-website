What the forms collect

The quote form collects first name, last name, phone, email, project type, project details, how you want to be contacted, and up to three optional files (PDF, JPEG, JPG, or PNG). The trade-partner form collects company, contact name, email, phone, trade, an optional license number, whether general liability and workers’ compensation are in force, and notes. The careers form collects name, email, phone, a trade or role, and a note. There are no open jobs behind that form.

A hidden field labeled “company website” is a spam trap. People using the form leave it blank. It is not used to identify you.

Why it is collected

This draft says the information is used to answer the inquiry, to consider a trade for future bids, or to hold a careers note until the owner decides whether to keep it. It is not required to browse the rest of the site.
